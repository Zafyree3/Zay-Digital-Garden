Canvas is a built in [[Topics/Obsidian\|Obsidian]] [[Topics/Plugins\|Plugin]] that allows you to organise/display your notes as a [[Flowchart\|Flowchart]].

You can drag in note, create cards with tag, paste in images, and link all of these up to each other as you see fit to better understand the relation of the notes.
