Emergence is about a pattern/structure that grows over time. The goal is not to pre-determine anything, let the notes and files itself determine the organisation. 

It can be thought of like a tree, where the branches are notes that form. Over time, clusters of notes will form so that emergent insights can be discovered.

Emergence can allow you to have serendipitous insight based on my own intuition.
