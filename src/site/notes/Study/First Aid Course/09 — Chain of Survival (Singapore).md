# Chain of Survival (Singapore)

The Chain of Survival refers to a sequence or chain of events that must promptly occur to improve the chances for survival and recovery for victims of cardiac arrest.

1. **Prevention** — prevention is better than intervention. A healthy lifestyle reduces the risk of heart disease.
2. **Early activation and AED** — prompt emergency response activation and use of an AED improves survival rates.
3. **Early CPR** — CPR must be initiated as soon as possible. Brain damage begins in 4–6 minutes if blood flow to the brain is not resumed.
4. **Emergency medical services (EMS)** — EMS professionals provide critical medical interventions and transport to hospital.
5. **Early defibrillation** — using an automated external defibrillator to shock the victim's heart improves survival rates significantly.
6. **Advanced cardiac life support (ACLS)** — initiated promptly at the hospital.
7. **Recovery** — ensuring a good survival outcome after cardiac arrest requires continued community and medical support.
