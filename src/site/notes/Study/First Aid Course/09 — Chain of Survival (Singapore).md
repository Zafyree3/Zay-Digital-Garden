# Chain of Survival (Singapore)

The Chain of Survival refers to a sequence or chain of events that must promptly occur to improve the chances for survival and recovery for victims of cardiac arrest.

1. **Prevention** — prevention is better than intervention. A healthy lifestyle reduces the risk of heart disease.
2. **Early activation and AED access** — call 995 and get an AED if visible and nearby, follow the SCDF dispatcher's instructions.
3. **Early CPR** — CPR must be initiated as soon as possible. Brain cells start dying within 4–6 minutes of cardiac arrest.
4. **Early defibrillation** — apply the AED to the casualty and be ready to defibrillate.
5. **Emergency medical services (EMS/ambulance)** — quick access to the scene and transport to hospital makes a difference to survival.
6. **Advanced cardiac life support (ACLS)** — begun at the hospital (IV/IO medication, oxygen via BVM and ventilators).
7. **Recovery** — continued community and medical support (social services, physio/occupational therapy, nutrition guidance) to rehabilitate the casualty.
