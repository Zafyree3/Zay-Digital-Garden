# Post Incident Procedures

## After Successful Resuscitation
- If there is return of spontaneous breathing and circulation, do not move the casualty unless there is immediate danger. Maintain the casualty in a supine position, check for any other injuries, and continue to monitor the casualty periodically.

## Gather History and Provide Assurance
- While waiting for the arrival of SCDF paramedics, provide assurances to the casualty (if conscious) and family members according to RMG Service Standards. Take the initiative to provide extra care for the casualty by finding out their medical history. Communicate and provide reassurance to the casualty and family members to reduce their anxiety, fear, or panic. In some circumstances, bystanders may also require assurances that the situation is under control.

## When the Paramedics Take Over
- When the paramedics take over the casualty, they may ask a series of questions or a summary of events that have occurred:
  - Time of collapse
  - Whether an AED was used and how many shocks were given
  - Any previous medical history (if known)
