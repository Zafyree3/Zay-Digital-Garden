# Abdominal Thrust (Heimlich Manoeuvre)

> [!tip] Abdominal Thrust _(Heimlich Manoeuvre)_
> - **Signs & Symptoms of Choking** _(complete foreign body airway obstruction)_
>   - Unable to speak
>   - Unable to breathe
>   - Unable to cough
>   - May be cyanotic (blue)
> - **Assessment**
>   - Ask: "Are you choking?"
>   - If the casualty is unable to speak, breathe or cough, tell the casualty that you can help
> - **Procedure of the Manoeuvre**
>   1. Stand behind the casualty
>   2. Position yourself with your feet shoulder-width apart
>   3. Put your arms around the casualty's abdomen
>   4. Locate the navel with your ring finger and place 2 fingers above the navel
>   5. Make a fist with the other hand, thumb in palm
>   6. Place the thumb side against the casualty's abdomen, above the 2 fingers
>   7. Lean the casualty forward while maintaining the fist against the abdomen
>   8. Grasp the fist with the other hand
>   9. Give **quick inward** and **upward** abdominal thrusts into the abdomen
>   10. Perform continuously until the foreign body is expelled or the casualty becomes unconscious

> [!info]- Chest Thrusts on a Pregnant or Obese Casualty
> 1. Stand behind the casualty
> 2. Position yourself with your feet shoulder-width apart
> 3. Place your arms under the casualty's armpits
> 4. Make a fist with one hand, thumb in palm
> 5. Place the thumb side on the middle of the casualty's sternum
> 6. Grasp your fist with your other hand and bring yourself close to the casualty before giving quick backward thrusts
> 7. Perform continuously until the foreign body is expelled or the casualty is unconscious

> [!danger] Unconscious Choking
> 1. Support and position the casualty lying on their back on a firm flat surface
> 2. Activate EMS
>    - If there are bystanders, ask them to call 995 for SCDF EMS and another to get an AED
> 3. Place the heel of your palm on the lower half of the sternum and put your other hand on top of the first hand, fingers interlocked
> 4. Start 30 chest compressions
> 5. Perform head-tilt, chin-lift manoeuvre to open the airway
> 6. Open the mouth to check for visible foreign bodies while maintaining the head tilt
> 7. If a foreign body is seen, use the index finger to dislodge it (finger sweep)
> 8. Look for one successful chest rise by attempting one ventilation via a BVM (bag-valve-mask)
> 9. If there is no resistance, the airway is clear
> 10. Otherwise, reposition the casualty's head and re-attempt to ventilate
> 11. If there is still resistance, perform 30 chest compressions
> 12. Check the mouth for any foreign body and reassess airway patency
> 13. Once the airway is clear, check for normal breathing and pulse
> 14. If both breathing and pulse are absent, start CPR
> 15. If the pulse is present but the casualty is not breathing, perform rescue breathing at 12 breaths per minute (1 breath every 5 seconds)
> 16. Give one breath and count "2-a-thousand", "3-a-thousand", "4-a-thousand", "5-a-thousand"
> 17. After performing rescue breathing, reassess for normal breathing and pulse
> 18. If both breathing and pulse are present, monitor the casualty until help arrives
