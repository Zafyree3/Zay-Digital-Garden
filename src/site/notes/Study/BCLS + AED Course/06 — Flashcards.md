# BCLS + AED — Flashcards

#flashcards

## DRSABC (adult)
What does DRSABC stand for?::Danger, Response, Shout (call 995), AED (fetch it), Breathing (check), Compressions (start CPR)
At which step do you tap the shoulders and ask "Hello! Hello! Are you OK?"?::R — Response
At which step do you check the carotid pulse?::B — Breathing check (together with rise and fall of the chest)
What number do you call for an ambulance in Singapore?::995 (SCDF)
What is the adult breathing/pulse check time limit?::Not more than 10 seconds
Is gasping normal breathing?::NO — gasping is a sign of cardiac arrest; start compressions if unsure

## Adult compressions & ventilations
What is the adult CPR compression-to-breath ratio?::30 compressions : 2 breaths (1 cycle)
Where do you place your hands for adult chest compressions?::Heel of palm on the lower half of the sternum, avoiding the xiphoid process (inverted V)
What is the adult compression depth?::4–6 cm
What is the adult compression rate?::100–120 per minute
What does "complete chest recoil" mean?::Let the chest fully rise back after each compression
Where is the xiphoid process?::The small inverted-V bone at the bottom of the sternum — must be avoided
When giving mouth-to-mouth breaths to an adult, what do you do with the nose?::Pinch it closed; release between breaths
How long is each ventilation breath?::About 1 second — enough for visible chest rise

## SPA — when to stop
What does SPA stand for?::Signs of life, Paramedics arrive, AED analysing
Which SPA condition means the casualty coughs/breathes/has a pulse?::S — Signs of life returned
When the AED says "analysing", what must you do?::Stop CPR and stand clear — nobody touches the casualty
How often do you re-check pulse and breathing during CPR?::Every 5 cycles (30:2 × 5), max 10 seconds each check
If you are tired after ~100 compressions, what may you do?::Rest up to 10 seconds, then resume

## AED operation
What are the 3 dangers to check before using an AED?::Water, metal, flammable gases
What does "AED is here" mean when the 2nd rescuer arrives?::"AED is here, continue chest compressions" — do not interrupt CPR
What 5 chest-preparation checks are needed before pads?::Hair (shave), Jewellery (move away), Pacemaker (pads 4 fingers away), Medication patches (remove), Sweat (wipe dry)
Where does the right AED pad go?::Right chest, just below the collarbone
Where does the left AED pad go?::Left chest, just below and to the left of the left nipple
What phrase describes adult AED pad placement?::Like a seat belt — top right to bottom left
When the AED is analysing, what do you shout?::"Stay clear!" — with arms spread in a V
How do you press the shock button?::One finger on the orange button, sweep-check nobody touches, shout "Stay clear", then press
After a shock is delivered, what do you do?::Resume chest compressions immediately
What do you tell the paramedics at handover?::Name (if known), time of incident, time CPR started, number of shocks, which hospital
What do you do with the AED kit after the incident?::Clean it and return it to the owner (building management/security)

## Adult choking (FBAO)
What is the universal sign of choking?::Hands clutching the neck
What are the 3 signs of complete choking?::Cannot speak, cannot breathe, cannot cough
What do you ask a suspected choking victim?::"Are you choking?" (they may only nod)
Where does your fist go for the Heimlich manoeuvre?::Just above the navel (ring finger on navel, fist above it), below the xiphoid
What direction are Heimlich thrusts?::Inward and upward, hard and fast
What do you use instead of abdominal thrusts for obese/pregnant victims?::Chest thrusts — arms under armpits, fist on centre of sternum, thrust inward and backward
In the unconscious choking sequence SSCAV, what do the letters mean?::Support down, Shout 995+AED, 30 Compressions, Airway check + sweep, Ventilate
When is a finger sweep allowed?::Only when the foreign object is VISIBLE — never a blind sweep

## Rescue breathing rates (pulse present, no breathing)
What is the adult rescue-breathing rate?::12 breaths per minute (1 breath, count to 5-a-thousand)
What is the child (1–12) rescue-breathing rate?::20 breaths per minute (count to 3-a-thousand)
What is the infant rescue-breathing rate?::30 breaths per minute (1 breath, count "2-a-thousand")

## Infant CPR
What age is an "infant" for BCLS purposes?::Under 1 year old
How do you check an infant's response?::Tap the shoulders AND the sole of the feet; ask "Hello! Hello! Are you OK?"
Where do you check an infant's pulse?::Brachial pulse — inner upper arm, between elbow and shoulder (NOT carotid)
What is the infant compression depth?::About 1/3 of chest depth (~4 cm)
How do you find the infant compression landmark?::3 fingers (index, middle, ring) under the far armpit, slide to centre, lift the index finger up — compress with middle + ring
What is the infant compression-to-breath ratio for one rescuer?::30:2
What is the infant ratio for 2 trained healthcare providers?::15:2
How do you ventilate an infant?::Seal your mouth over the nose AND mouth, 1 second per breath

## Infant choking (FBAO)
What is the conscious-infant choking sequence?::5 back blows between the shoulder blades, then 5 chest thrusts on the lower sternum — repeat until expelled or unconscious
In what position is the infant for back blows?::Face down along your thigh/forearm, head lower than the body
In what position is the infant for chest thrusts?::Face up along the other leg, head still lower than the body
What finger do you use to sweep an infant's airway?::Little finger (pinky), only if the object is visible
In the infant SSCAV sequence, where do you check the pulse?::Brachial — NOT carotid
What is the infant unconscious-choking compression count before airway check?::30 compressions, then head-tilt/chin-lift and check the mouth
