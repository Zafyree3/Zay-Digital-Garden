# AED Operation and Defibrillation

BCLS + AED Course — module 2. Verified against the SRFAC BCLS+AED Provider Course Manual (Rev 1/2022), Ch. 3.

## Role of the 2nd rescuer (AED runner)

- Arrive on scene with the AED and announce: **"AED is here, continue chest compressions."**
- Handover of the casualty must not interrupt CPR — minimise interruptions when preparing the chest and placing pads.

## Before opening the AED — 3 dangers

1. **Water** — wet/sweaty surfaces conduct electricity; wipe chest dry.
2. **Metal** — move casualty off metallic surfaces (conducts current to the rescuer).
3. **Flammable gases** — fire/explosion hazard; move casualty away from gas or oxygen sources.

## AED operation sequence

1. Open the AED bag, **press the green button** to switch on, and listen to the voice instructions.
2. **Expose the chest** — put on gloves, use the scissors to cut the shirt in an **L-shape**: from the right sleeve to the middle, then down the middle to the bottom.
3. **Chest preparation — 5 checks:**
   - **H — Hair:** shave the pad sites (top right, bottom left) if chest hair is excessive (AED kit has a razor).
   - **J — Jewellery:** move chains/necklaces to the left, away from the pads.
   - **P — Pacemaker:** if on the right chest, place the pad at least **4 fingers' breadth** away, toward the under-arm area.
   - **M — Medication patches** (e.g. Salonpas/medicated plasters): remove and wipe off any residue.
   - **S — Sweat:** wipe the chest dry for pad adhesion.
4. **Apply the pads** following the picture on the pad itself — like wearing a **seat belt: top right → bottom left**:
   - Right pad: right chest, just below the collarbone.
   - Left pad: left chest, just below and to the left of the left nipple.
5. **Plug the cable connector** into the AED and follow further voice prompts.

## Defibrillation procedure

- **"ANALYSING HEART RHYTHM. DO NOT TOUCH THE CASUALTY."**
  - Stop compressions, spread arms in a **V** and shout **"Stay clear!"** — no one touches the casualty while it analyses.
  - If no shock advised → resume chest compressions.
- **"PRESS THE SHOCK BUTTON NOW"** (shock ready):
  - Place **one finger on the orange shock button**, sweep your other hand over the body to check **no one is touching** the casualty, shout **"Stay clear!"**, then press firmly.
- After the shock is delivered (or no shock advised) → **resume CPR immediately**.
- AED re-analyses every ~2 minutes — repeat the stay-clear routine each time it prompts.
- AED should remain connected to the casualty throughout.

## Handover to paramedics

Report (as taught): casualty's **name** (if known), **time of incident**, **time CPR started**, **number of shocks given**, and which hospital the casualty will be conveyed to — so the next of kin knows where to find them. *(Manual's official list: time of collapse, whether AED was used, how many shocks, previous medical history/medications if known.)*

## After the incident

- Assist paramedics until the casualty is loaded into the ambulance. AED pads stay on the casualty during transport.
- **Clean the AED kit** and return it to its owner (building management / security).
- Inform the facility/safety manager the AED was used (consumables + battery get replaced by owner/vendor).

## AED use on children

- Use **paediatric pads/mode if available** (ages 1–12).
- If only adult pads: ensure pads **do not touch**, at least **2 cm (≈2 fingers) apart** with front placement; otherwise place **front and back** (anterior-posterior: front pad on central sternum, back pad on upper back between the shoulder blades).
