# Infant CPR

BCLS + AED Course — module 4. Verified against the SRFAC BCLS+AED Provider Course Manual (Rev 1/2022), Ch. 6. Infant = under 1 year old.

Infants rarely arrest from a primary heart problem — arrest is usually secondary to respiratory illness or trauma, so pick up early signs of respiratory/circulatory failure.

## DRSABC — differences from the adult sequence

- **D — Danger:** same as adult; move infant only if the site is unsafe
- **R — Response:** tap the infant's **shoulders AND the sole of the feet**, ask loudly *"Hello! Hello! Are you OK?"* — no violent shaking (head/neck injury risk)
- **S — Shout:** bystander calls **995**; alone → call 995 yourself, speaker mode, follow the dispatcher
- **A — AED:** someone fetches an AED if one is within ~60 s walk; alone → do NOT leave the infant
- **B — Breathing:** look for **rise and fall of the chest** (≤ 10 s) + **brachial pulse**:
  - 2 fingers (index + middle) on the **inner upper arm, between elbow and shoulder**
  - Gentle pressure, checked together with breathing — max 10 s
  - ⚠️ Gasping is NOT normal breathing
- **C — Compressions (single rescuer):**
  1. Expose the chest; landmark on the **lower half of the sternum** at the nipple line
  2. Course method: **3 fingers (index, middle, ring) under the far armpit → slide to the centre of the chest**, then **lift the index finger up** — compress with the pulp of **middle + ring fingers**
  3. Depth: **~1/3 of the chest's front-to-back depth (~4 cm)**
  4. Rate: **100–120/min**; allow complete chest recoil
  5. Count aloud: *1&2&3&4&5& … 1&2&3&4&30&*

## Notes

- Ventilations: after 30 compressions, seal your mouth over the infant's **nose AND mouth** (or use BVM), 1 s per breath, watch for chest rise
- 2 trained healthcare providers: ratio becomes **15:2** (10 cycles in 2 min)
- Rescue breathing (pulse present, no breathing): **30 breaths/min** — 1 breath then count "2-a-thousand" (see [[Study/BCLS + AED Course/05 — Infant Choking (FBAO)\|05 — Infant Choking (FBAO)]])
- Continue until paramedics take over, AED prompts to analyse, or infant wakes/regains normal breathing
