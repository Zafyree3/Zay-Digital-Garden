# Adult Choking (FBAO)

BCLS + AED Course — module 3. Verified against the SRFAC BCLS+AED Provider Course Manual (Rev 1/2022), Ch. 5. FBAO = Foreign Body Airway Obstruction.

## Recognition

- **Universal sign of choking:** hands clutching the neck
- Ask the victim (from the front): *"Are you choking?"* — a choking victim cannot speak, but may nod
- **3 signs of complete obstruction:** cannot **speak**, cannot **breathe**, cannot **cough**
- Announce: *"I am a first aider, I will help you. I am going behind you."* → move behind the victim
- If the victim CAN cough/wheeze (partial obstruction) → do NOT interfere; encourage them to cough it out. Act only if they deteriorate.

## Conscious adult — Heimlich manoeuvre (abdominal thrusts)

1. Victim stands with feet **shoulder-width apart**; place your **dominant leg between** theirs
2. Wrap one arm around the front to the belly button — **ring finger on the navel**
3. Other hand makes a **fist with the thumb inside**; place it just above that point (≈ at the index finger of the locating hand)
4. Move the non-fist hand to the victim's **shoulder**, ask them to **lean forward**
5. Cover the fist with the other hand
6. Deliver **inward and upward** thrusts, **hard and fast**, until the object is expelled or the victim becomes unconscious

## Obese or pregnant victims — chest thrusts (NOT abdominal)

- Do NOT lean them forward
- Arms under the victim's armpits, encircling the chest
- Fist placed on the **centre of the sternum** (breastbone)
- Thrust **inward and backward**

## Unconscious victim — SSCAV

- **S — Support.** Lower the victim to the ground, flat on the back (firm surface)
- **S — Shout.** Shout for help: call **995**, get an **AED**
- **C — Compression.** Perform **30 chest compressions** (same technique as CPR)
- **A — Airway.** Head-tilt + chin-lift (course: "head tilt with distal grip"); open the mouth and **check for the foreign object**
  - If an object is **visible** → index finger **sweep** it out, place it beside the casualty
  - If **nothing visible** → repeat (C + A): another 30 compressions, re-check the airway
- **V — Ventilate** (once the object is removed): give **1 breath** with the nose pinched, watch for **chest rise**
  - No chest rise → **reposition the head**, try the breath again

## After relief — check breathing & pulse (3 scenarios)

Check for normal breathing and pulse (max 10 seconds):

1. **Breathing + pulse present** → casualty is stable; **leave in supine position** and monitor until help arrives
2. **No breathing, no pulse** → **perform CPR** (30:2)
3. **Pulse present, no breathing** → **rescue breathing (adult): 12 breaths per minute**
   - 1 breath over 1 second (nose pinched, watch chest rise)
   - Then count **"2-a-thousand, 3-a-thousand, 4-a-thousand, 5-a-thousand"** before the next breath
   - Repeat until **12 breaths** delivered in ~1 minute, then **re-assess** breathing and pulse
   - *(Rates differ by age: child 20/min, infant 30/min — covered in the infant module)*
