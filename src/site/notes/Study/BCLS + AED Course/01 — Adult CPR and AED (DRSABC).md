# Adult CPR and AED (DRSABC)

BCLS + AED Course — module 1. Verified against the SRFAC BCLS+AED Provider Course Manual (Rev 1/2022).

## DRSABC — the adult cardiac-arrest sequence

**D** — **Danger.** Check for hazards that could harm you or the casualty. Resuscitate where the casualty is found unless the site is unsafe (traffic, fire, etc.) — then move them to a safe, flat, open space first.

**R** — **Response.** Tap the casualty's shoulders firmly and ask loudly: *"Hello! Hello! Are you okay?"* Avoid violent shaking (injury risk, especially to head/neck).

**S** — **Shout.** Shout to the nearest bystander to call **995** for an SCDF ambulance. If alone: call 995 yourself and put the phone on speaker — the dispatcher will guide your CPR.

**A** — **AED.** Ask another bystander to find and bring the nearest AED. If alone, do NOT leave the casualty.

**B** — **Breathing.** Open the airway with a head-tilt (one hand on forehead) + chin-lift (two fingers under the bony chin). Check for **carotid pulse** with the chin-lift hand, and lower your head to watch the **rise and fall of the chest**. Max 10 seconds.
- ⚠️ **Gasping is NOT normal breathing** — it is a sign of cardiac arrest. If unsure, start compressions.

**C** — **Compressions.** Begin CPR (below).

## Chest compressions (C)

- **Ratio:** 30 compressions : 2 ventilations = 1 cycle
- **Hand position:** heel of one palm on the **lower half of the sternum** (breastbone), above the inverted V (xiphoid process — must be avoided); second hand on top, fingers **interlocked and lifted off the chest**
- **Posture:** elbows locked straight, shoulders directly over the chest, compress using body weight
- **Rate:** 100–120 per minute
- **Depth:** 4–6 cm (~1/3 of chest width); allow **complete chest recoil** after each compression
- **Counting:** count aloud — *1&2&3&4&5&, 1&2&3&4&10& … 1&2&3&4&30&*

## Ventilations (the "2")

- After 30 compressions, give **2 breaths, 1 second each** (enough for visible chest rise)
- With BVM: mask sealed over nose and mouth → squeeze bag
- Without BVM (mouth-to-mouth): **pinch the nose**, seal your lips over the mouth, blow for 1 second, release the nose between breaths

## When to stop — "SPA"

- **S** — **Signs of life** return: casualty coughs, breathes, or has a pulse
- **P** — **Paramedics** arrive and take over
- **A** — **AED** prompts to **analyse** the rhythm (also when it is charging or delivering a shock)

## Note

- **Re-check pulse and breathing every 5 cycles** (30:2 × 5), max 10 seconds each check; if unsure, resume CPR
- If tired after ~100 compressions, a rest of up to 10 seconds is allowed (preferably after 100 compressions), then resume
