# Print-on-Demand (POD)

## Definition
A fulfillment model where a product (e.g. a t-shirt) is printed **only after
a customer orders it**. No inventory, no equipment, no warehouse — you pay
per item at fulfilment time. Launch in 1–3 weeks vs 3–6 months for
traditional manufacturing.

## How it works
1. Design a shirt (Canva/Illustrator) → list it on a store/marketplace
2. A POD partner (Printful, Printify, or a local print shop like [[Wiki/entities/cottony\|cottony]])
   prints + ships each order
3. You keep the margin: per-unit USD 10–25, typical profit 30–50%

## Costs & platforms (Singapore, verified)
- Startup: SGD 100–200 minimum, SGD 700–1,500 comfortable (incl. ACRA
  registration ~SGD 115)
- Etsy (free, ~6.5% per sale) · TikTok Shop (6% referral) · Shopee/Lazada
  SG (2–5%) · Carousell SG (free, the local discovery channel) · Shopify
- Local per-piece printing: SGD 23–41/tee at [[Wiki/entities/cottony\|cottony]]

## Why it matters here
Zaf's shirt business is POD-first (no bulk). The model pairs naturally with
AI-assisted design and marketing — a thread to [[Wiki/concepts/agentic-ai\|agentic-ai]].
