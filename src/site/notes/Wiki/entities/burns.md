# Burns

## What it is
Heat or chemical injury to skin/tissue. Treatment priority: stop the burning
process, cool, protect, monitor.

## First aid (verified from SFA manual)
- **Cool with running water** — chemicals need **15–20 minutes or longer** of
  washing with large amounts of water
- **Cold compress: max 20 minutes at a time** — longer risks frostbite/nerve
  damage; stop cooling if the casualty starts shivering
- Monitor vital signs and level of consciousness regularly
- **Do not feed** a casualty who is not fully conscious

## General rules (add your instructor's specifics here)
- Remove jewellery/loose items before swelling
- Do not burst blisters or remove adhered material
- Cover with a clean, non-fluffy dressing
