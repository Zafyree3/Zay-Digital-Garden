# Hermes Agent

## What it is
Zaf's always-on AI assistant (by Nous Research), running 24/7 on his Linux
laptop as a Telegram gateway. It manages cron jobs (daily S&P briefing, idea
garden nudges), drives the Obsidian vault + published digital garden, and
maintains this wiki.

## What it does for Zaf
- Research + builds: shirt business research, DRM deep-dive, PKM setup
- Automation: vault maintenance, digital garden publishing, packing-list
  tracking, daily briefings
- Integration: reads/writes the Obsidian vault (Zaf's PKM), calls [[Wiki/entities/ollama\|ollama]]
  for local AI, publishes the website, files ideas into daily notes

## Why it matters
The orchestrator of Zaf's whole [[Wiki/concepts/agentic-ai\|agentic-ai]] stack — the "agent" in
"agentic". This wiki is its shared memory layer: it writes here so any
future session (or agent) can pick up context instantly.
