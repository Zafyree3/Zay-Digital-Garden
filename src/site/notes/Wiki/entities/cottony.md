# Cottony

## What it is
Singapore-based print-on-demand t-shirt service (cottony.sg). Verified
Aug 2026: no minimum order, transparent online pricing, printed in SG.

## Key facts
- Basic tees from **SGD 22.90**, premium organic (same-day) **SGD 32.90**,
  oversized **SGD 40.90**
- 5 working days standard, same-day options for selected products
- Methods: DTG, DTF, screen printing

## Why it matters
The anchor supplier for Zaf's [[Wiki/concepts/print-on-demand\|print-on-demand]] shirt business — local
speed (same-day/next-day) is the moat vs overseas POD.
