# CPR (Cardio-Pulmonary Resuscitation)

## What it is
Chest compressions (plus rescue breaths for trained providers) that manually
circulate blood when the heart has stopped. Buy time until an [[Wiki/entities/aed\|aed]] or EMS
arrives.

## Recognition — cardiac arrest
- Unresponsive + **not breathing normally** → cardiac arrest
- Call **995** (SCDF ambulance); get an [[Wiki/entities/aed\|aed]] if one is near

## Adult compressions (verified from BCLS manual)
- **Rate: 100–120 per minute**
- **Depth: at least 4 cm, not more than 6 cm**
- Complete chest recoil after each compression
- Straight elbows, shoulders directly over the chest, body weight for depth
- **Ratio 30:2** (compressions:breaths) for trained healthcare providers;
  check breathing/pulse after every 5 cycles, continue 30:2
- Lay rescuers: **hands-only CPR** (per SRFAC guidance, SFA ch8)
- Loud counting encouraged (1&2&3&4&5…)

## When to stop
AED arrives, EMS takes over, casualty wakes, or you're exhausted.
