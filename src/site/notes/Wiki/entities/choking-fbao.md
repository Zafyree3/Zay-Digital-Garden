# Choking / Foreign Body Airway Obstruction (FBAO)

## What it is
An object blocking the airway. Two severity levels need opposite responses.

## Partial obstruction (wheezing, coughing)
- **Do NOT interfere** — let the casualty cough the object out themselves

## Complete obstruction
- Can't speak / breathe / cough; may turn blue (cyanotic); universal distress
  signal = clutching the throat
- Immediate action: **back blows, then abdominal thrusts** (fist in the
  midline of the abdomen, thumb side in)
- **Chest thrusts instead of abdominal thrusts for pregnant or obese**
  casualties
- If the casualty becomes unresponsive → start [[Wiki/entities/cpr\|cpr]] sequence
  (BA ch5.6 — relief of unconscious FBAO)

## Why it links to the airway family
Same first step as everything else: clear + protect the airway, then breathing,
then circulation.
